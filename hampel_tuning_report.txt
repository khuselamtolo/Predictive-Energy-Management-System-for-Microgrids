HOW TO READ THIS
--------------------------------------------------------------
The best cell is the best against SIMULATED faults. If the injected
faults do not resemble the errors actually present, the ranking does
not transfer. Weight the near-optimal set and the detection limit more
heavily than the single winning cell, and treat the flag counts on the
untouched series as review candidates rather than confirmed errors.
